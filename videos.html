🔍 What to Look For

First 3 Seconds (Hook)

  • • What grabs attention?
  • • Text overlay or spoken?
  • • Problem or benefit lead?

Structure

  • • How long is it?
  • • Talking head or b-roll?
  • • Text on screen throughout?

Social Proof

  • • Testimonials included?
  • • Results/numbers shown?
  • • Before/after?

CTA

  • • Clear call to action?
  • • Urgency/scarcity?
  • • What offer?
