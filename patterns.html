Winning Ad Patterns

Extracted from top performers running 30+ days

🔍 How to Find Winners

  1. 1 Go to Meta Ad Library
  2. 2 Search "web design [city]" or competitor name
  3. 3 Filter: Active ads only
  4. 4 Ads running 30+ days = proven profitable

8 Patterns That Work

1. The "Calling All" Hook
"Calling all [SPECIFIC AUDIENCE]..."
"Attention [CITY] business owners..."

Why: Self-selection. Right people stop, wrong people keep scrolling.

AWU: "Calling all Gold Coast business owners who are tired of their website collecting dust instead of collecting leads."
2. The Contrarian Statement
"Your website doesnt need more traffic. It needs better conversions."
"Stop paying for ads to a broken website."

Why: Pattern interrupt. Challenges assumptions.

AWU: "You dont need a new website. You need a website that actually works."
3. The Specific Number
"312% more traffic in 90 days"
"From 2 leads/month to 15+"

Why: Specificity = credibility. Round numbers feel made up.

AWU: "One Gold Coast tradie went from 3 website enquiries a month to 19 after we rebuilt his site."
4. The Before/After Story
"[Client] was struggling with [problem].
Now they [result]."

Why: Relatable narrative. Shows transformation.

AWU: "6 months ago, Sarahs accounting firm had a website from 2010. Today? Ranks #1 for accountant Gold Coast and brings in 8 new leads every month."
5. The Value Stack
"Your website includes:
✓ Feature 1
✓ Feature 2
✓ Feature 3
No hidden fees."

Why: Reduces anxiety. Shows complete picture.

6. The Objection Crusher
"Think you cant afford a professional website?
Our payment plans start at $X/week."

Why: Addresses the reason they havent bought yet.

AWU: "I dont have $5,000 for a website. You dont need $5,000. Payment plans from $50/week."
7. The Scarcity Play
"Only taking 3 new clients this month"
"Offer ends Friday"

Why: Creates action. FOMO is real.

8. The Social Proof Dump
"500+ websites built"
"4.9 stars from 127 reviews"

Why: Reduces risk perception.

AWU: "100+ Gold Coast businesses trust All Webbed Up. 4.9 stars on Google. No lock-in contracts."

🎯 Quick Wins - Test First

  1. 1. Review screenshot ad — takes 5 minutes to make
  2. 2. Before/after carousel — use real client work
  3. 3. "Calling all" hook — simple copy change

Budget: $30-50/day split across 3 ads → kill losers after 3 days → scale winner
