🛠️ Tools & Automation (Remotion + Viral Research)

Fast, practical stack to go from offer → script → video → variations.

Remotion (recommended for templated assembly)

Best when your videos can follow repeatable templates

Feasibility

What Remotion is good for: code-generated videos where you swap text, hooks, screenshots, b-roll, and music without manually editing every time.

High leverage templates to build

  • • Audit montage: hook → 3 quick problems → solution → CTA
  • • Before/after carousel video: before screenshot → after screenshot → mobile view → CTA
  • • Stats card: number + proof + CTA (15s)
  • • Offer explainer: what you get stack + turnaround + CTA

Reality check

Remotion won’t replace a good human editor for meme-style timing… but it *will* let us crank out 30+ variations/month once templates exist.

Next step: if you confirm 8 offers + preferred “house style”, I can spec 3 Remotion templates and the data schema (JSON) to feed them.

Viral / Winning Video Discovery (what’s worth it)

Tools that surface ads that are actually running

Research

Free (must use)

  • • Meta Ad Library (active ads)
  • • TikTok Creative Center (top ads)
  • • Google Ads Transparency Center

Paid (if we want speed)

  • • Foreplay (organise swipe files)
  • • BigSpy / AdSpy (deep search, filters)
  • • Motion / VidTao style tools (varies by niche)

Rule: any ad running 30+ days is almost always profitable. That’s your “viral”. Not likes.

Transcription pipeline (fast)

Turn competitor videos into scripts + hooks fast

Workflow
  1. 1) Grab competitor videos (Ad Library / TikTok CC)
  2. 2) Transcribe (YouTube auto, Whisper, etc.)
  3. 3) Extract: hook, structure, proof, CTA
  4. 4) Rewrite into 3 variants per offer
  5. 5) Feed into editor/Remotion templates
