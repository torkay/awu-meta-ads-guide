📣 10 Proven Ad Frameworks

Each framework is backed by performance data. Click to expand for copy you can steal.

1

Direct Targeting

Call out your exact audience in line 1

Why it works:

CTR increases 2.5x when you directly address your target audience. Unqualified leads self-filter.

Copy to steal:

"Calling all Gold Coast business owners who need a website that actually brings in customers."

Based on: HubSpot direct targeting model • Source: DigitalMarketer

2

Problem → Solution

Agitate the pain, then present the fix

Why it works:

Problem-aware audiences convert at 3.9% CTR. Shows you understand their struggle.

Copy to steal:

"Your website is stuck in 2015. Your competitors aren't.

While you're losing leads to slow load times, businesses down the street are converting visitors 24/7."

Based on: Slack's "Break out of the inbox" campaign

3

Before/After Transformation

Visual proof of results

Why it works:

Visual transformations achieve 2.5% conversion rate. Reduces resistance by 40%.

Creative format:

Split image or carousel:
• Slide 1: "Before" screenshot (outdated site)
• Slide 2: "After" screenshot (modern redesign)
• Slide 3: Mobile view
• Slide 4: Client quote
• Slide 5: CTA

Based on: Squarespace portfolio ads

4

Social Proof Screenshot

Let reviews sell for you

Why it works:

Third-party validation = 2x trust. Shopping/reviews category achieves 4.13% CTR.

Creative format:

Screenshot your best Google review as the ad creative.

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
"Andy's team turned our outdated website into a lead-generating machine. Best investment we've made."

Based on: Pilot.com PR-as-creative model

5

Value Stack Image

Dense info = high B2B CTR

Why it works:

B2B audiences want information density. Shows exactly what they get.

Creative format:

Single image with checklist overlay:

Your new website includes:
✓ Custom design (not templates)
✓ Mobile responsive
✓ SEO optimized
✓ Hosting included
✓ Ongoing support

[Get Started - Free Quote]

Based on: Canva B2B ad format

6

Educational Hook

Teach something, then sell

Why it works:

Value-first content = 35% lower CPC. Positions you as the expert.

Copy to steal:

"3 things killing your website conversions:

1. No mobile optimization (70% of traffic)
2. Slow load time (53% leave after 3 sec)
3. No clear call-to-action

The fix? Simpler than you think."

Based on: Grammarly educational content model

7

UGC Testimonial Video

Authentic beats polished 4x

Why it works:

UGC outperforms polished content by 4x. Feels real, builds trust.

Video script (15 sec):

"Honestly didn't think a new website would make this much difference. Andy's team rebuilt ours in 2 weeks and our phone hasn't stopped ringing. If you're on the fence, just do it."

Format: iPhone selfie-style, real client

8

Scarcity / Urgency

Limited spots = faster decisions

Why it works:

Limited offers drive 25% higher conversion. Creates FOMO.

Copy to steal:

🚨 February Special - 3 Spots Left

We only take 5 new website projects per month to ensure every client gets our full attention.

February spots: ██████░░░░ 2/5 remaining

→ Custom design
→ 2-week turnaround
→ $500 off this month only

Comparison Table

Us vs. DIY builders

Why it works:

Honest comparison builds trust. Addresses objections head-on.

Creative format:

10

Results/Stats Highlight

Numbers grab attention

Why it works:

Specific numbers = credibility. People are drawn to percentages.

Copy to steal:

"Our clients see an average of:

📈 312% more website traffic
📞 5x more inquiries
⏱️ 2.1 second load times

Same business. Better website. Real results."
